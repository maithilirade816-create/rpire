📖 How to Connect Gmail & Calendar

Follow these steps to get Rpire working with your accounts.

🔗 Step 1: Connect Gmail

Rpire needs permission to read and draft emails on your behalf.

  1. Click the "Connect Gmail" button below.
  2. You'll be redirected to Google's permission screen.
  3. Select the Google account you want to connect.
  4. Review the permissions — Rpire only reads emails you explicitly allow.
  5. Click "Allow" to grant access.
  6. You'll be redirected back to Rpire.

📅 Step 2: Connect Google Calendar

Rpire needs access to your calendar to check availability and schedule meetings.

  1. Click the "Connect Calendar" button below.
  2. You'll be redirected to Google's permission screen.
  3. Select the same Google account you used for Gmail.
  4. Review the permissions — Rpire only reads your availability.
  5. Click "Allow" to grant access.
  6. You'll be redirected back to Rpire.

🚀 Step 3: Start Scheduling

Once both accounts are connected, you're ready to go!

  1. Go to the Scheduler tab.
  2. Paste an email thread where someone asks to meet.
  3. Click "Find Available Times".
  4. Rpire will suggest 3 times based on your calendar.
  5. Click "Confirm" on your preferred time.
  6. Rpire sends the calendar invite automatically.
