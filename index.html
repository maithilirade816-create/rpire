Frequently Asked Questions

Is Rpire free to use?

Yes — you get 50 free credits per month. After that, you can upgrade to Pro or buy extra credits.

What do credits cover?

Each action uses credits: 1 credit to triage an email, 5 credits to draft a reply, 10 credits to schedule a meeting.

Is my data safe?

Yes — all data is encrypted and we never store your emails. You approve every action before it happens.

What if I don't approve a meeting?

Nothing happens. Rpire only books meetings when you explicitly click “Confirm.”
